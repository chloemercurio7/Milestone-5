1. Delay Accumulation Across the Operating Day

Morning Schedule Adherence

Flights departing between 05:00 and 08:30 AM tightly hug the zero baseline. Aircraft parked overnight are unencumbered by upstream network delays.

Afternoon Cascading Variance

After 14:00, departure delay variance widens substantially. Multi-leg aircraft utilization compounds small morning hiccups into severe late-day departures.
