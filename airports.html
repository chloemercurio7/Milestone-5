Why Newark (EWR) Records Higher Delays

EWR experiences intersection constraints between parallel runway operations and high corporate hub banking from United Airlines. When convective weather halts Mid-Atlantic arrival gates, Newark ground hold durations escalate rapidly.

Why LaGuardia (LGA) Leads in Punctuality

LGA operates under federal perimeter slot controls that cap the total operations per hour, mitigating systemic over-scheduling and preserving high on-time reliability (81.1%).
